SCP Object: SCP-049

Experiment Title: Communication and Behavioral Observation

Researcher: Dr. Michael Carter

Date: 28 July 2026

Clearance Level: Level 2

Test Subject: Class-D D-4821

Experiment Objective

To determine whether SCP-049 can be engaged in prolonged verbal communication without initiating physical contact.

Experiment Procedure

Class-D D-4821 entered the containment chamber under remote observation. The subject was instructed to communicate with SCP-049 using a prepared questionnaire. Security personnel remained on standby throughout the experiment.

Results

SCP-049 remained cooperative for approximately six minutes before identifying the subject as infected with "The Pestilence." SCP-049 attempted to approach the subject but containment personnel intervened before physical contact occurred.

Conclusion

SCP-049 is capable of extended communication, but all future interaction must be conducted under enhanced security protocols due to the high risk of sudden hostile behavior.
